# Directions (studio)

Produce three genuinely different directions. Each must change composition thesis, not only accent color.

For each direction:

- Thesis (one sentence)
- Tone and memorable detail
- Risk if it fails
- Implementation consequence (stack, motion budget, imagery)

Record what this product must not look like. After a pick, write the chosen contract under `.codex/design/directions/` and continue to UX + `DESIGN.md`.
