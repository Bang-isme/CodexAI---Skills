# Fit Heuristics

Do not chase a year-dated trend list. Fit the surface mode and the product.

## When quiet chrome wins

`operate` and `read` surfaces: low decoration, high grouping, durable tokens. Fashion textures (glass, aurora, brutalist posters) usually fail task completion.

## When atmosphere wins

`persuade` and `experience` surfaces: one distinctive material or type decision is enough. More than one competing “signature” is noise.

## Recurring traps

- Gradient mesh behind unreadable type
- Bento grids with twelve equal cells
- Fake 3D heroes that hide the CTA
- Dashboard widgets that all use the same card and sparkline

If a device or aesthetic is used, it must serve the composition thesis in `grammar.md`.
