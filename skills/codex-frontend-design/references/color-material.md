# Color and Material

Pick a color strategy from `grammar.md` before tokens. Material is the surface treatment, not a second brand.

## Strategy before hex

- Incumbent tokens win.
- One accent for `operate`. `persuade` may add a second accent only for proof or price.
- Neutrals must include text, muted text, border, fill, and inverse.

## Material

Choose one primary material: flat ink, paper, soft elevation, or hard editorial rules. Mixing glass, neon, and heavy drop shadows in one view is noise.

Elevation exists to separate grouping, not to decorate every card. If every panel has a shadow, none is important.

## Contrast

Primary text on the chosen fill must meet WCAG for the surface. Decorative large type is not a substitute for a readable body style.
