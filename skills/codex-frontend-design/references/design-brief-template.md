# Design Brief (fast path)

Fill this in five lines, then implement. Do not interview.

1. Audience: who uses this and in what moment
2. Job: the one action that must succeed
3. Tone: one extreme (editorial, industrial, warm, brutal, luxury, playful)
4. Memorable: the one detail a visitor will recall
5. Constraints: stack, a11y, existing tokens, what must not change

Reject: Inter on white, blue-to-purple hero, three equal feature cards, centered-everything, lorem CTAs.
