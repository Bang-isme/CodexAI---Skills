# Design Brief (fast path)

Fill this in five lines, then implement. Do not interview.

1. Audience: who uses this and in what moment
2. Job: the one action that must succeed
3. Tone: a product-fit quality (editorial, industrial, warm, calm, playful, etc.)
4. Memorable: the one detail a visitor will recall and why it fits
5. Constraints: stack, accessibility, existing tokens, content/proof, what must not change

Treat familiar combinations as a prompt to explain their product job, not an automatic ban. Prefer real product copy; label unavailable claims or proof as placeholders.
