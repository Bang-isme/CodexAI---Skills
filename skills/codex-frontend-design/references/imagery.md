# Imagery and Assets

## Provenance

Record source for every visual asset: user-supplied, generated in-repo, licensed font, or named open set. Do not invent product photos or customer logos.

## Role of images

- `persuade`: image must prove the claim or show the product in use.
- `operate`: imagery is rare; icons must be a single set and optically aligned.
- `read`: diagrams over decoration.
- `experience`: atmosphere is allowed; still caption or alt for meaning.

## Icons

One optical size and stroke model per surface. Mixing filled Material icons with thin stroke icons fails grouping.

## Alt text

Meaningful images get specific alt. Decorative images get empty alt. Icon-only buttons get an accessible name.
