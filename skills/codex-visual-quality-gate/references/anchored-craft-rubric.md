# Anchored Craft Rubric

This 1–10 scale is an optional secondary note. It never replaces capture evidence, and it never overrides a blocking defect.

## How to score

Scores are integers. Write the concrete gap next to any score below 8.

- `8` means only minor nits remain: a spacing rhythm, a secondary label, or a non-blocking alignment. The primary task, type, and contrast already hold.
- `7` means the surface is below an `8` bar. Name the gap. Do not describe a 7 as almost an 8.
- Never round `7.5` up. A reviewer who is between 7 and 8 records `7`.

## What the score cannot do

A rubric score never overrides blocking clipping, overflow, contrast, or missing-capture defects. Those fail the gate even when a reviewer would have scored the page high. Missing browser evidence stays `DEGRADED`. Do not average blocking defects into a passing score.

Use the score only after the route, state, viewport, and capture key are recorded. A number without that anchor is not a review.
